CAMPUS DATA GOVERNANCE & SECURITY PROTOCOL

Institutional Data Privacy & User Protection Manifesto

Legal guidelines, privacy safeguards for students & faculty, and protection of campus intellectual property

1. User Data Privacy & Confidentiality Rights (Students & Faculty)

CampusNotify is built on uncompromising principles of user data dignity. All personal information collected (Full Name, Username, Contact, Password, and Profile Bio) is governed by strict technical controls:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in raw plaintext. Client-side cryptographic processing (SHA-256 with institutional salting) ensures that even administrators or databases cannot inspect your true password.
  • Data Minimization Principle: We only collect information strictly necessary for academic circular distribution and community collaboration. No private biometric data, financial information, or off-campus location tracking is collected.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization / No Third-Party Trackers: Student and faculty data is never sold, leased, rented, or distributed to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • Right to Erasure & Rectification: Every user holds the fundamental right to edit their profile bio at any moment and request complete account purging or comment deletion through the Super Admin desk.

2. Company & Campus Intellectual Property Protection (Our Platform)

To safeguard our institution, protect students from misinformation, and ensure institutional reliability:

  • Anti-Forgery Circular Governance: Only verified institutional authorities (Super Admin or explicitly delegated faculty) hold circular issuance clearance. This prevents forged or fabricated notices from circulating.
  • Multi-Account Device Approval Shield: While multiple accounts can be created on a single device, newly registered accounts are quarantined under Pending Approval until approved by Campus Administration to prevent spam bots and impersonation attacks.
  • Institutional Moderation Authority: The Super Admin holds exclusive restriction authority to moderate and delete any abusive or non-compliant comments or files to protect campus integrity.

3. Google Cloud Firebase Architecture (Cloud Firestore Security)

When connected to Google Cloud Firebase:

  • Strict Role-Based Security Rules (firestore.rules): Enforces that only authorized accounts can write to official collections, guaranteeing server-side access control.
  • Encrypted Transport (TLS 1.3): All real-time synchronizations between client browsers and Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted in transit.

Verified Cryptographic Security All messages, student queries, and group discussions are encrypted on your local client device using AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations) and a unique initialization vector (IV) per payload before transmission.

🛡️ Privacy Shield Architecture:

  • Peer Anonymity: Other students and viewers only see your public handle (e.g. @scholar) and your chosen photo/avatar. Your private personal email and phone number are never leaked.
  • Super Admin Exclusive Audit: Only the institutional Super Administrator possesses verified regulatory audit access to authenticate real enrolled identities for campus compliance.
  • Zero Plain-Text Interception: Even if cloud packets are intercepted, adversaries see only randomized AES ciphertext.
